## Layout Contract

- Design from usable height and safe areas, not width breakpoints alone.
- Keep current equity, exact next risk, durability, and capture action in the first
  60% of every supported portrait viewport.
- Let the monument adapt: cinematic on first visit/achievement, compressed on
  repeated use, and never taller than the operational information it supports.
- Bottom navigation and sticky submit bars must reserve real content padding and
  never overlap each other, the keyboard, or scroll targets.
- Long surfaces need section routes/disclosures and a persistent local section
  index rather than one 2,000+ px page.
