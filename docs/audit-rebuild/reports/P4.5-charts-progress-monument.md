# P4.5 Charts, Progress, and Monument

Captured: 2026-07-12 PDT

State: `EVIDENCE_READY`

Evidence ID: `EV-P4-VIS-005`.

## Monument

Retain it. It is the only visually ownable asset and the user's explicit preference.
Its problem is not style; it is operational dominance, redundant raster delivery,
and motion density. The target monument has three states:

1. **Ceremonial:** full-height reveal for first visit, milestone crossing, or manual
   expand.
2. **Operational:** compressed 150-190 px trail with equity, current segment, and
   next checkpoint.
3. **Risk:** desaturated/storm state that yields hierarchy to drawdown and next risk.

## Charts

Current charts use very small axes/legends and occasionally mount at invalid sizes.
On mobile, each chart needs one question, a one-sentence answer, a full-screen detail
mode, accessible summary/table, fixed min dimensions, and no hover-only meaning.
The probability cone must disclose model version and assumptions adjacent to it.

## Progress

The trail, Summit Tracker, roadmap, and celebration repeat milestone state. Keep one
canonical progress object and derive three distinct representations: compact Home
segment, detailed Insights roadmap, and ceremonial crossing. Realized progress and
simulated arrival must never share the same visual certainty.

## Checkpoints

Capital milestones alone are insufficient. Add operational checkpoints such as
backup verified, model version adopted, review completed, and data-quality coverage,
but visually separate them from capital progress so discipline cannot be mistaken
for wealth outcome.
