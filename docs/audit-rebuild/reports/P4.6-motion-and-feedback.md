# P4.6 Motion and Feedback

Captured: 2026-07-12 PDT

State: `EVIDENCE_READY`

Evidence ID: `EV-P4-MOTION-006`.

## Inventory

The app combines Framer Motion transitions with trail drawing, shimmer, breathing
glow, ring pulses, campfire flicker, god rays, falling particles, cloud flashes,
bar growth, spring tabs, sheet spring, card entrances, confetti, button scaling, and
toast timers. Reduced-motion handling exists for only part of the Home spectacle.

## Judgment

Motion is concentrated on decoration rather than state explanation. Continuous
effects compete with a high-stakes value/risk surface, increase decode/paint work,
and make reduced motion incomplete. Meanwhile, the important transitions—local save,
queued sync, upload acknowledgement, merge, conflict, and recovery—lack a coherent
motion grammar.

## Target Motion Grammar

- 80-120 ms press/focus response.
- 160-220 ms route/sheet transition with no arbitrary stagger before data appears.
- One 2-3 second nonblocking save/sync receipt that can be reopened from System.
- Monument reveal only on meaningful lifecycle events; no simultaneous shimmer,
  particles, rays, flashes, and pulse loops.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all nonessential transforms, path drawing,
  confetti, particles, shimmer, pulse, and parallax.
- Motion never communicates the only evidence of success/failure.
