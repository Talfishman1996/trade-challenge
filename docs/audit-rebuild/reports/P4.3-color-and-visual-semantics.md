## Current Semantics

- Blue means navigation, primary form action, slider value, focus, toggle, and FAB.
- Green means profit, Win, positive analytics, and the primary Log Trade action.
- Amber/gold means monument achievement, win rate, risk, and some projected states.
- Cyan means next milestone and trail progress.
- Red/rose means loss, destructive action, drawdown metrics, and higher risk.

The palette is attractive in isolation but semantically overloaded. The most serious
example is the bright green Log Trade CTA during a 60% drawdown; green reads as
approval/success while the user is in a risk warning state. Amber simultaneously
means celebration and exposure.
