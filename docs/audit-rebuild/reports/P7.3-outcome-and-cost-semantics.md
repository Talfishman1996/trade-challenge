# P7.3 Outcome and Cost Semantics

Captured: 2026-07-12 PDT

State: `EVIDENCE_READY`

Evidence ID: `EV-P7-SEM-003`.

## Trade Record Truth

```text
plannedRiskDollars     canonical model output at decision time
plannedGrossReward     plannedRiskDollars (fixed gross 1:1)
grossPnl               price/fill P&L before fee/funding
fees                   signed realized exchange fees/rebates
slippageCost           decision/arrival benchmark cost
funding                signed payment (+ received, - paid)
netPnl                  grossPnl - fees - slippageCost + funding
plannedOutcome          WIN | LOSS | BREAK_EVEN | UNCLASSIFIED
```

Gross 1:1 means a planned full Win is `+1R` and planned full Loss is `-1R` before
execution costs. It does not promise net `+1R/-1R`.

## Break-Even

`BREAK_EVEN` is a modeled outcome and remains BE even when costs make `netPnl < 0`.
Resolved win rate excludes BE:

```text
resolvedWinRate = wins / (wins + losses)
P(BE) = 0.10
P(Win) = 0.90 * resolvedWinRate
P(Loss) = 0.90 * (1 - resolvedWinRate)
```

Financial summaries use net P&L; behavior/outcome summaries use the explicit outcome
enum. A user-entered zero with no classification remains `UNCLASSIFIED`, not
silently Win, Loss, or BE.

## Execution Validity

Preferred projections require losses capped near planned R. Whole-wallet cross-
margin liquidation is outside this validity boundary because loss can consume more
than planned risk. The app must not display approved-model deadline probabilities
for such a configuration. This opens `F-MODEL-CRITICAL-003`.

The venue cost model is versioned and includes notional, stop width, maker/taker fees,
entry/exit slippage by size/side, and signed funding. Historical actual fields are
never replaced by a planning profile.
