## Convergent Product Patterns

1. **Capture and analysis are different modes.** Strong journals allow fast entry,
   then richer notes, charts, tags, psychology, and reports later.
2. **Retrieval is global and composable.** Symbol, side, tag, duration, and date
   filters operate across journal/report contexts rather than one local card list.
3. **Qualitative data needs a review output.** Screenshots, emotions, and mistakes
   matter when they produce a ranked leak, report card, or next-session focus.
4. **Costs and execution context are first-class.** Fees, liquidity, risk, target,
   stop, MAE/MFE, and entry/exit context prevent P&L from becoming the only truth.
5. **Mobile scope is intentionally bounded.** A useful mobile product does not need
   every desktop analytic control at the top level.
6. **Reviewer access is permissioned and revocable.** If critic/reviewer access is
   later built, read-only scope is safer than sharing the owner's canonical write
   surface.

## TradeVault Differentiation

The defensible core is not more statistics. It is a tightly integrated sequence:
versioned next-risk decision, explicit realized outcome, immediate local receipt,
per-record cross-device durability, transparent consequence, and psychologically
usable progress toward a custom capital target. None of the reviewed official
materials establishes this exact risk-curve-plus-durability loop as its primary
mobile job.

## Implications

- Do not build a feature marketplace or configurable dashboard before ledger truth.
- Add day/session review only after trade retrieval and immutable risk provenance.
- Keep screenshots, tags, and psychology fields, but score their data completeness
  and turn them into a small number of evidence-backed review prompts.
- Treat automatic exchange import as a later independent reconciliation pipeline;
  it must not silently overwrite manually recorded decision context.
- Reviewer/prototype mode should use deterministic synthetic scenarios and read-only
  controls, matching the owner's stated reviewer/critic audience.
