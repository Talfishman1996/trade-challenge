## Disposition Rules

- **Essential:** directly protects or advances Act, Confirm, Understand, or Review.
- **Supportive:** useful only when attached to an essential job and truthful data.
- **Ceremonial:** emotional identity, motivation, or delight; never decision truth.
- **Redundant:** another surface already owns the same question.
- **Misleading:** current capability or certainty is overstated.
- **Missing:** required to complete a core workflow safely.
- **Deferred:** not justified before the trust foundation exists.

## Missing Foundation Features

| Missing feature | Priority | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Local commit receipt and pending-sync queue | critical | The user must know the trade is accepted without waiting for cloud. |
| Per-record durability status | critical | Global Synced cannot prove a specific trade reached the shared store. |
| Conflict inbox/resolution | critical | Concurrent edits need visible, deterministic ownership. |
| Versioned model/execution snapshot | critical | Historical risk and R truth cannot change with a formula import. |
| Canonical Win/Loss/BE semantics | critical | Zero-P&L must not be both win and loss. |
| Net execution-cost fields/model | high | Gross 1:1 is not net 1:1 after fee, slippage, and funding. |
| Search and saved review views | high | Lifetime journal retrieval cannot rely on scrolling. |
| Post-save consequence receipt | high | Show accepted record, equity delta, next risk, and direct Edit. |
| Backup health and restore drill | high | Export availability is not proof a usable recovery copy exists. |
| Data/model provenance panel | high | Every projection must expose assumptions and model version. |
| Data sufficiency indicators | high | Behavior conclusions need coverage/sample-size boundaries. |

## Telegram Decision

Telegram does not belong in the critical path. Before reliable authenticated sync,
adding it creates another credential, delivery, privacy, duplication, and false-
acknowledgement surface. Defer it until the outbox is authoritative. If later
justified, its first scope should be one-way operational alerts only: failed sync,
backup overdue, risk-control override, or weekly review ready. Telegram must never
be the canonical ledger, a write path, or proof that cloud persistence succeeded.

## Portfolio Principle

Complexity is justified behind the scenes where it improves correctness,
diagnostics, simulation fidelity, and recovery. The repeated mobile surface should
expose only the state and action needed at that moment, with deeper evidence one
level away. This is not minimalism; it is deliberate allocation of complexity.
