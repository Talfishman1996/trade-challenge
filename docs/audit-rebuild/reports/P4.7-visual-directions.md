# P4.7 Visual Direction Alternatives

Captured: 2026-07-12 PDT

State: `EVIDENCE_READY`

Evidence ID: `EV-P4-DIR-007`.

## Direction A: Monument Cinematic

Full-bleed mountain, gold/cyan trail, large portfolio value, layered atmospheric
motion, dark glass cards. Highest emotional impact and closest to current identity.
Risk: operational information remains secondary and asset/motion budgets stay high.

## Direction B: Risk Instrument

Compact status header, oscilloscope-like charts, strict grid, dense tabular values,
signal colors, minimal image presence. Highest scan speed and easiest performance
budget. Risk: looks interchangeable with crypto dashboards and discards the strongest
identity asset.

## Direction C: Summit Ledger

Recommended hybrid. A compressed, state-responsive mountain sits behind the current
segment and expands ceremonially. Below it, an editorial expedition ledger uses
Barlow for language, JetBrains Mono for values, mineral surfaces, bone text, summit
gold only for achieved checkpoints, cyan for active controls, and explicit record
receipts. History reads as a field journal; Insights reads as evidence plates rather
than a widget wall.

This direction preserves emotional ambition while moving complexity into drill-down,
data provenance, and recovery. It is intentionally not “less”; it gives every layer
a job.
